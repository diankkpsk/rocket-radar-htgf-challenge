## Refresh and replay

A live scan checks robots rules, uses fixed public HTTPS endpoints, limits request counts and response sizes and checks eligible public hosts before connecting. The KIT adapter starts at the canonical KIT FORGE magazine. The public HTGF index is parsed deterministically for local search. Its published content signal permits search and declines AI input and model training. The connector uses no model or training service. Publisher errors stop a scan and leave its previous results available. [HTGF robots policy](https://www.htgf.de/robots.txt) · [KIT robots policy](https://www.kit-forge.de/robots.txt) · [TUM robots policy](https://www.tum.de/robots.txt)

The original source publication date, the date of the described event, first observation and retrieval have separate fields. Unknown values are null. Re-fetching refreshes the applicable evidence retrieval time. Supplementary sources that were reviewed separately carry their original clocks into subsequent scans. Rerunning a local replay reads the captured observations from disk. Source HTML, scripts and private contact details are excluded from the application export.
